Production workflow

A simple path from script to final cut.

  1. Upload or paste your screenplay.
  2. Add character reference images.
  3. Break the screenplay into scenes.
  4. Generate prompts and create clips with a video model.
  5. Attach your clips and export the project for editing.
CINE AI organises production; it does not yet generate video itself. A video-generation model/service must be connected for that capability.
